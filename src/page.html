01

Invoice pipeline

Simulates the suppliers, carriers and customs broker of a cosmetics packaging importer: about a hundred invoices a week from abroad, as PDF files, a quarter of them scanned, plus the Brazilian duty statements, broker bills and trucks. It reads them back into two CSV files and costs every part landed at the Moty plant, in reais.

  • 981 of 1,003 invoices read with nothing left open; 22 went to a person.
  • II, AFRMM and Siscomex in the cost; IPI, PIS, COFINS and ICMS kept apart as credits.
  • Ten kinds of process error planted, each with an honest look-alike.
Python · pdfplumber · Tesseract · Prometheus · Grafana · Docker

02

Invoice anomalies

Learns to find errors at each stage of the process: a price above the contract, more billed than received, the same bill twice, a freight charge not on the rate card, a vendor with no order, an account the vendor master does not hold, a duty at a rate not in force, a broker fee above its contract.

  • Rules, Isolation Forest, ECOD and LightGBM compared; every run recorded in MLflow.
  • On invoices it never saw, the clerk's checks lead at 0.53 average precision, LightGBM at 0.03. Every interval overlaps, so the gap is not proven.
  • The honest result was published, not the flattering one.
pandas · LightGBM · PyOD · MLflow

03

Landed cost dashboard

This dashboard, on the real run of the other two. Invoices shows the week the desk is working; Anomalies shows the scores from project 02, our errors apart from the client's; Finance shows the month being closed, final apart from provisional. The assistant names the panel to open, code fills it, and a sentence carrying a number nobody computed is thrown away.

  • Three views, the same layout and the same guard on each.
  • Every figure traces back to the lines it came from.
